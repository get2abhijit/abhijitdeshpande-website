FIELD NOTES / PERSONAL · 3 NOV 2026

Husband and dad, before everything

This site lists projects, roles and experiments. The most important roles in my life don’t have a project page.

It would be easy to read a personal website as a summary of what someone does. Software engineer, cricket coach, performance analyst, builder of side projects: those are all true, and they are all here. But they come second.

The order matters

I’m a husband and a dad first. For me that isn’t a slogan; it’s a scheduling rule. Side projects get the time that is left over, not the time that was promised to someone at home. It is one reason I like small, self-contained projects: they can be picked up for an evening and put down again without anything falling over.

Where the two meet

Sometimes family and building overlap, and those are the projects I enjoy most. My wife Nihira is a musician, and building nihiramusic.in for her is one of the things I’m proudest of — not because it was technically difficult, but because it mattered to someone I care about. FamDeck, the family dashboard I’m working on, comes from the same place: a small attempt to make everyday family life a little more organised.

Why say it at all

Because a website like this one can quietly suggest that the projects are the point. They aren’t. They are what I do with the time around the things that matter more.
