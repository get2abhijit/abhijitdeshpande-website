Norway and Denmark with the family

Thirteen days in August: Bergen, Voss and the Nærøyfjord, Oslo, a night at sea and Copenhagen. Most of what follows is practical — the things I wish we had known before we went.

When
14–26 August 2026
Who
The three of us

Bergen

We flew from Frankfurt via Amsterdam and landed late, so there was not much to do on the first day. The tram took us from the airport to the main bus station, and our hotel, Scandic Ørnen, was directly across the road from it. Checking in late was no problem. The room was small but good, with a view of the mountains — and of the bus station.

More first impressions of Bergen to come.

Walk up Fløyen, ride down

We walked from the lower Fløibanen station up to Mount Fløyen and took the funicular back down. At a relaxed pace, with rests and photo stops, the climb took about an hour and a quarter. It was 15–16 °C with light rain; a light jacket and ordinary sports shoes were enough.

The first fifteen minutes are the confusing part: several trails branch off and the Fløyen signs are hard to spot. After that the signposting improves and the route is easy to follow. At the top there are toilets, drinking water, a playground and cafés, and a busy viewpoint over the harbour and the city.

Walking up and riding down worked well for us as a family: the satisfaction of the climb, without the whole descent on foot.

VilVite

VilVite, Bergen’s science centre, has interesting exhibits for every age group, including a robot-programming lab where the staff help you along. It works as a family visit, or as a parent-and-child outing.

Ulriken, just before the fog

The next day we hiked up Mount Ulriken, about an hour and a quarter, with excellent views on the way. The summit was clear when we arrived and completely fogged in fifteen or twenty minutes later. We had coffee and snacks at the restaurant at the top, then hiked back down. If Ulriken is clear when you are ready to go, go.

A bus tip

Bus 610 between Kuven and Bergen Busstasjon is a pleasant, scenic ride through small towns and countryside — a relaxed option on a wet day. The 600 is not the same: it mostly uses highways and tunnels.

Voss

The train from Bergen arrived on time. Scandic Voss is right beside the station and let us leave our luggage before check-in, which matters when you arrive with bags in the morning.

We took the Voss Gondola up to Hangurstoppen and back down — panoramic views without a long climb. Lunch came from the El Caminito Mexican food truck, and it was good. In the afternoon we walked to Bordalsgjelet and back: about 5 km in total, not each way. The evening was the hotel sauna and pizza from Alpino.

Plan dinner in Voss. The choice is smaller than in Bergen, and the Indian restaurant we had in mind, Amraj, was closed that first evening.

Two nights in Voss is more than sightseeing alone needs. For us it was the right buffer: we were still tired from Bergen before the big Flåm day.

Flåm and the Nærøyfjord

This was the big day, and it ran on a tight timetable:

  • Voss 08:25 to Myrdal 09:17.
  • Flåm Railway, Myrdal 09:30 to Flåm around 10:28 — only about 13 minutes to change trains.
  • An excursion from Flåm up to the Stegastein viewpoint.
  • The 15:00 boat from Flåm through the Nærøyfjord to Gudvangen.
  • The bus from Gudvangen back to Voss, and dinner at Amraj, open this time.

Seats on the Flåm Railway are not assigned, so board promptly if you care which side you sit on. Our planning notes said the left-hand side, facing the direction of travel, has the better views on both trains. Leave time in Flåm to find the pier, and be there 20–30 minutes before the boat leaves.

More on the railway, Stegastein and the fjord cruise to come.

Kayaking, then the train to Oslo

On our last morning in Voss we rented kayaks from Outdoor Norway (opens in a new tab) for an hour. It was very exciting and everyone enjoyed it — if you have the time in Voss, do it. Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult, so my wife and our daughter shared a kayak.

Voss, from the kayak.

Before the train, Deli de Luca opposite the station had good food to take on board. The evening train was punctual, clean and almost empty — but it reached Oslo around midnight. With a child, think about the arrival time, not just the train.

Oslo

We stayed at Bjørvika Apartments, and finding the apartment took about 45 minutes. The map pin led to the front entrance of a different office, the address we were given led to a back entrance, and the instructions for the key box were unclear. If you rent an apartment, ask for exact entrance and key-box directions before you travel, especially for a late arrival. The kitchen was useful for breakfast.

The next day started late and stayed relaxed. We walked the harbour, past the Parliament and up to the Royal Palace — the central sights are close enough together that we did not need public transport at all. Favourite stops on the walk, later.

On the second full day we took the museum island tour: the Kon-Tiki Museum, the polar ship museum and the Norwegian Folk Museum. More to come.

A night at sea

We sailed from Oslo to Copenhagen overnight on DFDS’s Nordic Pearl, in a sea-facing cabin with breakfast included. Dinner on board was Italian, and the evening was fun: music and a lively atmosphere, plus the sauna and pool. We arrived in Copenhagen at 10 in the morning. More later.

Copenhagen

We stayed in Sydhavnen for three nights. More later: getting around from the hotel, the three days, Tivoli.

We flew home on 26 August via Amsterdam to Frankfurt.

Planning it like a project

I planned the trip in a travel handbook I built for us: bookings, the route and a guide to each hotel in one place. Did it help on the road? What would you change?

What I’d tell another family

  • Kayak in Voss if you have the time. Under-16s share a kayak with an adult.
  • Walk up Fløyen and ride the funicular down. Expect the first fifteen minutes to be confusing.
  • Go up Ulriken while it is clear; it can fog over in a quarter of an hour.
  • VilVite has something for every age, robot programming included.
  • The Myrdal change is about 13 minutes, and Flåm Railway seats are unassigned.
  • The Skyss app would not register a German mobile number for us. The ticket machines work, and Bybanen tickets must be bought before you board. A Bergen ticket does not cover Voss.
  • Plan dinners in Voss and check opening times.
  • Check what time a late train arrives, and get exact entrance and key instructions for any apartment.
